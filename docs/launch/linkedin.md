# LinkedIn

When AI agents deliver work across teams or suppliers, the expensive moment is not the generation —
it is the dispute over **which version was delivered, who accepted it, and whether the record was
changed**.

agentbridge is an open-source (MIT) delivery-evidence layer: signed delivery/acceptance records,
versioned revisions, key history, and an offline-verifiable evidence pack. It does not claim to
judge quality or resolve disputes; it makes the facts checkable by the receiving side.

Current status: pre-1.0, local CI evidence, no independent audit. Looking for design partners with
real cross-org AI delivery workflows.
